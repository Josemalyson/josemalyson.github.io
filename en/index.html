How I work

  1. 01

    Diagnosis

    Map the flow that exists today: who approves, where the agent acts, and which evidence is missing.

  2. 02

    Pilot with one squad

    One closed flow with a single squad: intention, spec, bounded execution, and verification.

  3. 03

    Playbook

    What survived the pilot becomes a reproducible step, with a version and a date.

  4. 04

    Scale

    The same loop on other squads. What breaks comes back as a guardrail in the retro, not as a silent exception.

The method is the guardrail + retro loop. Each run leaves evidence (test, review, policy). What fails becomes a rule on the next cycle.
